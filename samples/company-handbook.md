# Company Handbook

Employees receive twenty five vacation days per year. Vacation requests must be approved by your manager at least two weeks in advance. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of five days.

Remote work is allowed up to three days per week. Employees working remotely must be reachable on Slack during core hours, which are 10:00 to 16:00 Israel time.

The office is located on the fourth floor. Visitors must sign in at the reception desk and wear a guest badge at all times.

New laptops are issued every three years. If your laptop breaks, open a ticket with the IT helpdesk and you will get a replacement within two business days.
